Dear Friend,

This is a chain letter, started by Daniel Woc in Guatemala on October 7, 2026, as part of a school art project. It is also a way of gathering a flock of humans, like the flocks of birds and fish that learned to move together to ward off the falcon.

Please forward this letter to 7 people you are close with, within 7 days. Send it to each person separately. A flock only forms if we learn to build community, dare I say love. Add your first name to the list at the bottom of the letter, and reply to tell me who you sent it to.

You also have a task: choose a video from your camera roll of a fond memory that you are comfortable sharing, and send it to me at a.flock.of.birds.777@gmail.com with a short reason why. It can be anything; a puppy chasing a ball across a field, a beautiful sunset, a song at a party. Please let me know what friends you've sent the letter to as well :)

In return, you will receive a link to the finished piece, where you can see everyone's contributions moving together as a flock.

One reader forgot to send this on, and her tea went cold. A week later she found it, sent it, and a stranger answered. This is a story, not a fact, but it could be true.

Your video may appear in a class art project. Tell me if you'd rather it didn't.

If you break the chain, nothing will happen to you. But one bird will be missing, and flocks with gaps are easier to scatter.
